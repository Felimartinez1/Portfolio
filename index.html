Featured case study

Lebane · Data & AI Engineer

Lena: AI Reporting Agent

One message, the whole report. Ask for what you have in mind and get a client-ready report with your logo, colors, typography, site photos and numbers, in the time it takes to make a coffee.

Problem
At month-end, developers have to send construction progress reports to dozens of buyers: copying numbers from five screens into PowerPoint and fighting with the logo. An entire afternoon of stressful work.
What I built
An AI agent that generates and iterates on reports and analytics from natural language. You say what you want to see and who it's for; it picks the metrics, cross-references them and writes the narrative. Show it any piece of your brand (a logo, a typeface or a reference report) and every report comes out in your style.
Impact
An afternoon of work resolved in a single message. Democratized data access across non-technical teams and drastically cut ad-hoc request turnaround times.
